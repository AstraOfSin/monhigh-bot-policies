MONHIGH Guild Bot

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Who operates the bot

MONHIGH guild leadership operates MONHIGH Guild Bot for the MONHIGH Aion 2 Discord community. This policy covers the guild setup and role-management bot distributed in the MONHIGH Easy Discord Bot Setup package, and these policy webpages.

Questions and data requests go to the MONHIGH Guild Master or a Co-Leader through Discord. See Your choices and data requests below.

2. Information the bot processes

Discord supplies information needed for the bot’s features. Depending on the action, this includes:

  • Your Discord user ID, username or display name, guild membership, bot-account status, assigned roles, and permissions.
  • Server, channel, category, role, and message IDs and names; channel topics and permissions; and guild configuration.
  • Slash-command inputs, including the member and rank selected by an administrator, and activity-button selections.
  • Limited message history in configured onboarding channels. During setup, the bot checks recent messages for its own setup markers to avoid duplicate welcome, rule, guide, and role-panel posts.

The bot does not continuously collect or archive ordinary conversations. It does not record voice or video, retrieve your Discord password, or collect Aion 2 account credentials, payment information, or your IP address through Discord.

3. How information is used

The bot uses this information to create and manage guild channels and roles, check administrative permissions, assign Trial on joining, send a welcome message and direct message, apply activity-role choices, perform staff-requested rank changes, and troubleshoot operation.

A rank change creates a message in the configured mod-log channel naming the affected member, selected rank, and administrator. Discord may also record role and server changes in its own audit log. These records support guild administration and accountability.

The current bot does not use member information for advertising, profiling, AI training, or automated promotion decisions.

4. Where records are kept

The current bot has no separate member database and does not write a member roster, activity choices, or promotion history to a local data file. During operation, its Discord library holds member, role, channel, and interaction information in memory.

Roles are stored in Discord. Bot-generated welcome messages, setup posts, and rank logs are also stored in Discord. Welcome posts can be visible to members who can view the welcome channel. Rank logs are posted to the configured staff channel and are visible to people with access to it, including server administrators.

The operator’s computer or bot host stores configuration such as the server ID, role and channel settings, and the bot’s access token. Diagnostic output may contain server or user identifiers, names, command-related errors, or operational details. The current bot has no custom analytics or persistent logging database; a hosting provider may retain console output according to its own settings.

5. Access and sharing

Discord receives the requests needed to operate the bot. Authorized guild staff can see administrative records according to server permissions. If a hosting service runs the bot, that service may process configuration and diagnostic information needed to provide hosting.

We do not sell member information or share it for advertising. Information is shared only as necessary with service providers operating the bot, as expressly directed by the user, or when required by law. Server messages and assigned roles remain visible to the people allowed to see them in Discord.

Discord’s own handling of platform data is described in its Privacy Policy.

6. Retention and deletion

Information held in the bot’s runtime memory is temporary and is released when that runtime ends. Roles and bot-generated messages stored in Discord can remain after the bot restarts, is removed, or a member leaves the guild; leaving does not automatically erase historical welcome messages or rank logs.

Guild leadership retains bot-generated records only as needed for the bot’s stated administrative purposes. On a valid deletion request, leadership will promptly remove personal information from bot-generated records it controls and any retained diagnostic output, unless applicable law requires retention. Leadership will also remove unnecessary API data when the bot stops operating.

The current bot has no automatic retention timer or deletion command. Deletion is handled by leadership. Discord’s own records, audit-log retention, and any data outside the operator’s control are governed by Discord’s policies.

7. Your choices and data requests

Click an activity button again to remove that optional role. You can stop using bot commands at any time. Ask leadership to remove other bot-managed roles or to address your bot-related information.

To request access, correction, or deletion, send a Discord direct message to the MONHIGH Guild Master or a Co-Leader with the subject MONHIGH Bot Privacy Request. Include your Discord user ID, the affected server, and what you want accessed, corrected, or deleted. Do not send a password, token, or identity document.

Leadership can be identified by guild roles in the MONHIGH server’s member list. If direct messages are unavailable, ask an Officer for a private contact route. Before leaving the server, keep a working leadership contact if you expect to need help afterward.

We may verify the request using your Discord account to prevent unauthorized changes. We will respond and act promptly, subject to applicable legal requirements. Available privacy rights depend on your location. A request concerning Discord’s own account or platform records should also be made directly to Discord.

8. Security

Access to bot configuration and diagnostic output is limited to the people operating the bot. Access to staff channels is controlled by Discord permissions. Discord API connections use encrypted transport. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Never share your Discord password or the bot’s token. Report suspected unauthorized access to the Guild Master or a Co-Leader so leadership can investigate and take corrective action.

9. These webpages

These static policy pages contain no forms, embedded advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, cookies set by the pages, or browser-storage code. Images and styles are served with the pages; no external font service is used.

The website’s hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, browser details, and access logs, to serve and protect the site. If the pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub’s processing is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

10. Age and policy updates

The bot is intended for people eligible to use Discord under its minimum age requirements. We do not knowingly collect information from children who are too young to use Discord. Report any such concern privately to leadership.

This page will be updated when the bot’s features or data practices change. Material changes will also be announced in the MONHIGH Discord. The revised date appears at the top of this page.
