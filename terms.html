Terms of Service

Effective and last updated: October 3, 2026

1. About the bot

MONHIGH Guild Bot is operated by MONHIGH guild leadership for the MONHIGH Aion 2 Discord community. It helps set up guild channels and roles, welcomes new members, assigns a Trial role, lets members select activity roles, and lets authorized administrators manage guild ranks.

This is a community bot. It is not an official product of Discord or the publisher of Aion 2, and it is not endorsed by them.

2. Using the service

When you use the bot’s commands or role buttons, you agree to these terms. You must meet Discord’s minimum age requirements for your country and follow Discord’s Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, applicable laws, and MONHIGH’s guild rules.

The bot is provided without a fee. Guild leadership may change, suspend, or discontinue its features. Use is limited to servers authorized by the bot operator.

3. Respect and permitted use

Respect every guild member. Do not use the bot or its features to harass, threaten, discriminate, scam, expose private information, impersonate others, spam, or disrupt the community. Competitive play and constructive feedback are welcome; personal attacks and bullying are not.

Do not attempt to bypass role restrictions, gain unauthorized permissions, exploit the bot, obtain its credentials, or interfere with its operation. Do not submit passwords, bot tokens, payment details, or other sensitive information to the bot.

4. Roles and staff decisions

Activity buttons let you add or remove optional roles such as Raids, PvE, PvP, Dungeons, Crafting, Open World, Event Ping, and LFG Ping. Selecting an activity role does not grant a leadership position or guarantee participation in an event.

New human members receive Trial when the bot is online and configured with the necessary permissions. Guild ranks and promotions are controlled by leadership. In the current bot, setup and rank commands require Discord Administrator permission. The bot does not automatically decide promotions or resolve disputes.

Guild leadership may restrict bot access or membership for rule violations. Raise moderation concerns privately with an Officer, Co-Leader, or Guild Master. No rank grants an exception to MONHIGH’s respect standard.

5. Privacy

The Privacy Policy describes the Discord information processed by the bot, the records it creates, and how to request access, correction, or deletion. Discord also processes information under its own policies.

6. Availability and responsibility

The bot depends on Discord, its host, and the permissions configured by server administrators. It may be unavailable or fail to complete an action. There is no guarantee of continuous availability, successful role assignment, or error-free operation.

Administrators are responsible for checking permissions and the results of setup or rank changes. To the extent permitted by applicable law, the bot is supplied as available without additional warranties. Nothing in these terms excludes rights or responsibilities that cannot lawfully be excluded.

7. Changes to these terms

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. Material changes will also be announced in the MONHIGH Discord. Review updated terms before continuing to use the bot. If you do not agree, stop using its commands and buttons and contact leadership about removing optional roles.

8. Contact

For bot support or questions about these terms, send a Discord direct message to the MONHIGH Guild Master or a Co-Leader. You can identify them by their guild roles in the MONHIGH server’s member list. If direct messages are unavailable, ask an Officer in the guild for a private way to contact leadership.
